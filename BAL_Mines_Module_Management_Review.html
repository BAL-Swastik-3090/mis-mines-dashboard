MIS Dashboard — 9 sections

AI Insights & Reality Check
Live
 LLM narrative across 6 domains replaces manual review notes; digest pre-generated at 07:00 so the 08:00 management review opens instantly instead of waiting on generation. Reality Check computes run-rate vs required-rate per KPI and flags ACHIEVABLE / STRETCH / NOT FEASIBLE; exception alerting, rolling 7-day trend and today-vs-yesterday comparison. Runs on the internal LiteLLM gateway — API cost only, no external AI licence. Breakdown hours are converted to lost-ore MT for cost context.
Equipment Utilization
Live
 MTTR / MTBF, breakdown drill-down and fuel LPH per machine on demand — no manual breakdown register. Excavator and tipper availability & utilisation computed from SAP PM notification durations, with per-machine trend charts. Breakdown-hour visibility enables targeted maintenance; rupee benefit to be measured.
Fuel Management
Live · Phase 1
 Fleet fuel level, refill and drain events and a 7-day consumption trend pulled straight from telematics — replaces manual fuel registers. Per-vehicle LPH, tank level, estimated hours remaining and low-fuel banding across the fleet. Drain / pilferage detection is live; quantified saving arrives with Phase 3 costing.

Way Forward — Blockers · Dates · Funds · Actions

Constraints & Blockers

  • EV vehicle API not releasedBlocked
    Vendor has not delivered the EV vehicle API, so Electric Vehicles Tracking cannot start. Entirely vendor-gated — needs commercial escalation, not development.
  • SAP PM time columns pendingActive
    Preventive-maintenance duration is date-level only. SAP CoE is adding start and completion time columns; until then OEE PM hours are approximate.
  • Excavator loss-hour fields blankActive
    Weekly-off, no-plan and planned-shutdown fields are newly added and not being filled, which overstates OEE availability. Operations discipline issue, not a development one.
  • Two new modules have no scopePlanned
    Mines Costing and AMIRA Accounting are both approved in principle but undefined. AMIRA in particular has no identified source system. Neither can be estimated or scheduled until Finance & Accounts confirms scope.
  • Vehicle ID mismatchBlocked
    Telematics device IDs do not match physical registration numbers, so vehicle names display inconsistently in Fuel Management. A mapping sheet is required from the mines mechanical team.
